This website uses NHS.UK frontend components, clear headings and keyboard-accessible links. You can use your browser to enlarge the text.

## Help using the website

If you need information in another format or have difficulty using a page, call [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) or ask at reception.

Online booking and registration are separate services with their own accessibility information.
