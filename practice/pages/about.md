The Range Medical Centre is an NHS GP surgery at **121 Withington Road, Whalley Range, Manchester, M16 8EE**.

Our purpose-built medical centre opened in 2002 for the partnership previously based at Park Surgery on Alexandra Road South. It provides disabled access, meeting and health promotion areas, parking and emergency ambulance access.

We aim to provide high-quality healthcare that meets each patient's individual needs.

## Care Quality Commission

The Care Quality Commission inspects GP services in England. [Read the CQC information and inspection reports for The Range Medical Centre](https://www.cqc.org.uk/location/1-581450737).
