## Order a repeat prescription

The easiest way to order is through your [NHS account or NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/account/). Your account shows your repeat medicines and doses so you can choose what you need.

You can also:

- leave a written repeat prescription request at reception
- fill in the slip from your last prescription and post it to the surgery, with a stamped addressed envelope
- order through your pharmacy **only if you are over 75 or have a condition that affects your ability to manage your medicines**

Telephone requests are only accepted from very elderly or infirm housebound patients who have an agreed telephone arrangement recorded by the practice.

## How long it takes

You can usually collect your prescription **2 working days** after ordering it.

Choose a pharmacy to collect from. This is called your nominated pharmacy. You can change it in the app or website where you order, at the surgery or at a pharmacy that accepts repeat prescriptions.

## Prescription questions

For questions about your prescription, phone our pharmacist **after 10am** on [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099). Your local pharmacist can also answer questions about medicines.

If you have a repeat prescription, we will contact you when you need a medication review.
