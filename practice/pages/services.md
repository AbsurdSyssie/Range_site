Our clinical team provides appointments and ongoing care, including:

- diabetes, asthma and blood pressure reviews
- blood tests and blood pressure checks
- cervical screening
- contraceptive services
- childhood vaccinations and immunisations
- flu and travel vaccinations
- new patient health checks

Tell reception what your appointment is for so we can allow the right amount of time with the right member of the team.

[Book an appointment](/appointments/) or call [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099).
