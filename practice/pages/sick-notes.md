---
title: Sick notes
---

To request a sick (fit) note, phone [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) or visit the surgery between **10am and 6pm**.

A doctor may call you or arrange an appointment. If they have enough information to issue a first sick note, we aim to have it ready within **2 working days**. For an extension to an existing note, we aim for **5 working days**.

If the note relates to a hospital procedure, contact the hospital doctor responsible for your care.

[Read the NHS guide to fit notes and self-certification](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/getting-a-fit-note/).
