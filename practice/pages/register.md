## Join the surgery

[Register with The Range Medical Centre online](https://register-with-gp.ht1.uk/?gpCode=P84039&gpName=The%20Range%20Medical%20Centre).

If you cannot register online, visit the practice between **10am and 6pm** to collect a paper registration form.

Call [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) if you need help checking whether your address is in our catchment area.

It is helpful to have your NHS number when registering. You can [find your NHS number on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/online-services/find-nhs-number/).

## Temporary registration

If you are staying near the surgery for up to 3 months, ask reception about registering as a temporary resident. You remain registered with your permanent GP.

[Read the NHS guide to registering with a GP surgery](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/how-to-register-with-a-gp-surgery/).
