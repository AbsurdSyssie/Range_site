## Extended-hours appointments

Extended-hours appointments are also available at the practice:

- Tuesday: 7:30am to 8am and 6:30pm to 7:30pm

Through Manchester Primary Care Partnership, appointments are available Monday to Friday, **6:30pm to 8pm**, and Saturday and Sunday, **9am to 4pm**. Contact reception during opening hours to book. [Find out about Manchester Extended Access](https://manchesterpcp.co.uk/services.php).

## When we are closed

If you need urgent medical help when we are closed, use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111). Call [999](tel:999) in a life-threatening emergency.
