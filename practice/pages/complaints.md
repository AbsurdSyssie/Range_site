We use patient feedback to improve our services. Phone [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) or speak to reception to give feedback.

## Make a complaint

If possible, speak to the person involved first. Many problems can be resolved quickly at the time.

For a formal complaint, write to:

The Range Medical Centre<br>
121 Withington Road<br>
Whalley Range<br>
Manchester<br>
M16 8EE

Please tell us what happened as soon as you can. Complaints should normally be made within 12 months of the event, or within 12 months of becoming aware of it.

## Complaining for someone else

We normally need the patient's written consent to discuss their care with another person. Ask reception for a complaints form and statement of authority. If the patient cannot consent because of illness or an accident, explain the circumstances in your letter.
