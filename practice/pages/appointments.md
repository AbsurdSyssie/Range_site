## Contact reception or try GP Triage

For appointments, phone reception on [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) during surgery opening times. You can also try [GP Triage](https://gptriage-prod.azurewebsites.net/?ods_code=P84039&website_address=https://www.therangemedicalcenter.uk). If it is unavailable, phone reception.

## Routine appointments

You can request a routine appointment by:

- trying GP Triage
- use your [NHS account or NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/account/)
- use [Patient Access](https://patient.emisaccess.co.uk/Account/Login)
- phone [0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099) or speak to reception in person

Consultations may be by phone, face to face or by video.

## Evening and weekend appointments

Evening doctors' appointments are available at the surgery on Wednesdays from **4pm to 7:50pm**.

We also work with [Manchester Extended Access](https://manchesterpcp.co.uk/services.php) to offer GP, nurse and health care assistant appointments in the evenings and at weekends. Ask reception to arrange a suitable time and location. These appointments are at another local service, not your usual practice.

## Change or cancel an appointment

You can check or cancel appointments in your NHS account or Patient Access. You can also call reception. Please tell us if you cannot attend so we can offer the appointment to someone else.

## Home visits

If you are too ill to attend the surgery, call to request a home visit, preferably before **10am**. A doctor will discuss your request and the most suitable care with you.
