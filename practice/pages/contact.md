## Phone

Call reception on **[0161&nbsp;870&nbsp;8099](tel:01618708099)** during surgery opening times.

For urgent help when we are closed, call [111](tel:111) or use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/). In a life-threatening emergency, call [999](tel:999).

## Getting here and access

There is a patient car park and street parking nearby. The surgery has disabled access. You can [plan a public transport journey with Transport for Greater Manchester](https://tfgm.com/plan-a-journey).
